About RE:LABS

We start with the question.

A creative thinking studio in Chennai, and the experimental arm of RE_WORKS.

Most briefs arrive with the answer already inside them: a new logo, a campaign, an app. RE:LABS takes the answer out and looks at the question underneath. Often it is a better one.

We work across brand strategy, creative communication, sustainable materials, design innovation, AI-assisted experiments, digital experiences and new business concepts. The disciplines change from project to project. The method does not.

RE  Return, rebuild, rethink, reimagine.

LABS  Experiment, discover, make, transform.

RE:LABS is part of the RE_WORKS family. RE_WORKS is the brand and strategy partner clients hire; RE:LABS is where that work is tested, made and published first.
